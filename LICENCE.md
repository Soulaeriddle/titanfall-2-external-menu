## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Loadout Switcher — swap pilot and titan presets mid-session
- HUD Viewer — visualize on-screen status panels
- Replay Notes — record and review movement runs
- Playback Speed — adjust practice playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Pilot Catalog — browse skins, camos, banners, and callsigns
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize pilot cosmetics
- Motion Tracker — improved visibility of nearby markers

### Aimbot
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any map
- Movement Helper Notes — chain wall-runs and slides in the editor
- Frame Buffer Notes — study complex maneuvers with stable timings
